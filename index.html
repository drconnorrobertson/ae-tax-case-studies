Featured Case Studies

A closer look at ten recent engagements, spanning diverse industries, property types, and planning strategies.

Cost Segregation

Multi-Family Portfolio Owner Accelerates $1.2M in Depreciation

A real estate investor holding a portfolio of four apartment complexes (112 units total) across two states had been using straight-line depreciation since acquisition. Our cost segregation study reclassified 38% of the total basis into 5, 7, and 15-year property classes, unlocking substantial first-year deductions and freeing capital for additional acquisitions.

$1,218,400 Accelerated Depreciation
$341,152 Year-One Tax Savings
  • Reclassified site improvements, parking, and landscaping to 15-year life
  • Identified $420K in personal property (appliances, flooring, cabinetry)
  • Applied bonus depreciation to all short-life components
  • Coordinated with CPA for proper Form 3115 filing
Entity Restructuring

Physician Group Practice Saves $127K with S-Corp Election

A four-physician medical group operating as a multi-member LLC was paying self-employment tax on the full net income of $1.4M annually. After analyzing reasonable compensation benchmarks and state-specific rules, we restructured the practice as an S-Corporation. The change reduced self-employment tax exposure and allowed the physicians to retain more earnings as distributions.

$127,300 Annual Tax Reduction
14:1 Engagement ROI
  • Converted from multi-member LLC to S-Corporation
  • Set reasonable compensation using BLS and MGMA data
  • Established accountable plan for unreimbursed expenses
  • Implemented quarterly estimated tax schedule to avoid penalties
Prior Year Amendments

Real Estate Investor Recovers $89K from Three Prior Tax Years

A client with seven rental properties had been filing returns without professional tax advisory for the previous three years. A lookback review revealed multiple missed deductions including depreciation errors, unclaimed repairs, and overlooked safe harbor elections. We filed amended returns for 2022, 2023, and 2024, recovering a combined $89,200 in overpaid federal and state taxes.

$89,200 Total Recovery
3 Years Amended Returns Filed
  • Corrected depreciation schedules using proper placed-in-service dates
  • Claimed de minimis safe harbor election retroactively
  • Recovered missed Section 199A qualified business income deductions
  • Applied partial asset disposition rules under Reg. 1.168(i)-8
STR Tax Strategy

Airbnb Host Offsets $215K in W-2 Income with STR Losses

A software engineer earning $340K in W-2 income purchased a short-term rental property and needed to materially participate to use the losses against ordinary income. We documented over 750 hours of material participation across property management, guest communication, and maintenance coordination. Combined with a cost segregation study, the client generated a paper loss large enough to offset a significant portion of W-2 wages.

$215,400 W-2 Income Offset
$60,312 Federal Tax Savings
  • Documented 750+ hours of material participation for STR exception
  • Completed cost segregation study on $485K purchase price
  • Applied 100% bonus depreciation to short-life components
  • Structured self-management to avoid passive activity limitations

  • Restructured practice as S-Corp with reasonable compensation analysis

Real Estate Professional Status

Spouse Qualifies as Real Estate Professional, Unlocking $174K in PAL Offsets

A married couple with $620K in combined W-2 income had accumulated $310K in suspended passive activity losses from their rental portfolio. The spouse who managed the properties full-time had never formally documented REPS qualification. We established a contemporaneous time log, verified the 750-hour and material participation requirements, and filed the election. The unlocked losses offset current-year income and produced a significant refund after amending the prior year return.

$174,200 Passive Losses Unlocked
$48,776 Combined Tax Refund
  • Established contemporaneous REPS time log for 1,100+ hours
  • Filed grouping election under IRC Section 469(c)(7)
  • Released $310K in suspended passive activity losses
  • Amended prior year return to capture additional deductions
Retirement Planning

Law Firm Partner Shelters $312K Annually with Defined Benefit Plan

A senior partner at a mid-size law firm earning $780K annually was maximizing a solo 401(k) but still facing a high tax burden. After evaluating the firm's employee demographics and cash flow, we designed a cash balance defined benefit plan layered on top of the existing 401(k). The combined contributions sheltered over $312K per year from current taxation, substantially lowering the partner's effective rate while building retirement assets.

$312,000 Annual Tax-Deferred Contribution
$109,200 Annual Tax Savings
  • Designed cash balance defined benefit plan with actuarial projections
  • Layered plan on top of existing 401(k) with profit sharing
  • Minimized cross-testing issues to limit required employee contributions
  • Projected $3.1M in additional retirement assets over 10 years
Equipment Leasing

Construction Company Deducts $438K in Equipment Through Section 179

A commercial construction company with $2.8M in annual revenue needed to replace aging heavy equipment. We structured the acquisition of three excavators, two loaders, and a fleet of service trucks to maximize Section 179 and bonus depreciation in the purchase year. The combined deductions offset a significant portion of the company's taxable income, resulting in an effective federal tax rate of just 14% for the year.

$438,200 Total Equipment Deductions
$131,460 Tax Savings
  • Applied Section 179 expensing to qualifying equipment purchases
  • Utilized bonus depreciation for assets exceeding Section 179 limits
  • Timed acquisitions before year-end placed-in-service deadline
  • Structured financing to preserve cash flow while maximizing deductions
Exit Planning

Business Owner Saves $620K on $4.2M Practice Sale Using QSBS Exclusion

The founder of a healthcare technology company planned to sell the business for $4.2M. The company had been structured as a C-Corporation from inception and held qualifying small business stock under IRC Section 1202. We verified QSBS eligibility, confirmed the five-year holding period, and ensured the active business requirement was met. The founder excluded a significant portion of the gain from federal taxation, resulting in $620K in tax savings compared to ordinary capital gains treatment.

$620,000 Tax Savings on Sale
$4.2M Business Sale Price
  • Verified IRC Section 1202 QSBS eligibility and holding period
  • Confirmed active business and gross asset tests were satisfied
  • Structured installment sale for remaining taxable portion
  • Coordinated with buyer's counsel on tax allocation under Section 1060
Multi-State Optimization

E-Commerce Business Reduces State Tax Burden by $67K Through Nexus Planning

An e-commerce business with $3.6M in annual revenue was filing income tax returns in 14 states based on outdated nexus assumptions. After conducting a nexus study and analyzing economic nexus thresholds, we determined the company only had filing obligations in 8 states. We also identified a more favorable apportionment method in two high-tax states. The combined savings from eliminating unnecessary filings and optimizing apportionment totaled $67,400 annually.

$67,400 Annual State Tax Reduction
6 States Unnecessary Filings Eliminated
  • Completed full nexus study across all 50 states
  • Eliminated filing obligations in 6 states lacking economic nexus
  • Switched to single-sales-factor apportionment in two key states
  • Implemented Voluntary Disclosure Agreements where past exposure existed
