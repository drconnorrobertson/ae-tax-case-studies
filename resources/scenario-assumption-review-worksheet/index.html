Illustrative Tax Scenario Assumption Review Worksheet

This worksheet is an educational reading tool, not a client case history. Use it to identify which assumptions in an illustrative scenario differ from your situation before asking an advisor to evaluate the concept.

Capture the starting facts

Write down the entity type, tax year, household income categories and ownership stated in the scenario. Identify assumptions supplied by the author and inputs omitted for simplicity. Do not fill missing information with a favorable assumption.

Trace the arithmetic

Separate the stated deduction from the estimated tax change. Check which rate was applied and whether the comparison includes fees, state treatment and future consequences. A simplified calculation explains a mechanism; it is not a complete return model.

Identify the gates

List the eligibility and limitation questions that must be answered before using the approach. Mark each one as supported, missing or requiring professional review. Matching a scenario's income level does not establish that its other facts match.

Create a review question

Describe what you want the advisor to test and provide the records needed. Ask for a comparison with the baseline alternative using your facts. Keep the scenario as background education rather than treating it as an implementation instruction.

Printable preparation worksheet

Use a private working copy to record the document location, outstanding question and person responsible for completing each item. These blank fields do not submit or store information online. Keep sensitive data in your secure records.

Before you request advice

Mark unresolved items clearly and explain any deadline that may affect the review. Ask for an individualized conclusion and a written work scope before relying on a recommendation. A completed preparation worksheet is evidence organization, not approval of a tax position.
